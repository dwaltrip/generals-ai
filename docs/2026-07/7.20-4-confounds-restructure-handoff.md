## Orientation

[`docs/confounds-framework.md`](../confounds-framework.md) was overhauled end to end this session.

- The classification is now more clearly grounded in the two contracts.
- §1's execution-site columns became three yes/no tests (alters training, alters the runtime path, alters what numbers mean)
- The old impacts section merged into §1, and the later sections were renumbered (verdicts §2, cures §3, worked examples §4).
- The intro and "The two contracts" were rebuilt.
- The confusing and inaccurate "generating rule" section was deleted.
- A new "Artifacts: run versus read" section defines the kind of "recorded numbers" and the subject/apparatus split.

A blind content-review ran on the final text (report: `tmp/content-review/2026-07-20-confounds-framework/confounds-framework.md`). Its three issues and two of its four flags were applied; the other two were settled by Daniel's call (the 7.20-3 link in the update note stands, and no em-dash pass).

One transferable lesson: most of this session's fixes, including the core ones, replaced execution-site language with contract-axis framing. The site framing doesn't map cleanly onto the edge cases and nuance we needed to illuminate, and the attempts to make it work caused continual issues.
