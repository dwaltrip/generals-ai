We are getting ready to build the first cut of "behavior goldens". These are golden tests for obs builder (`build_obs`) and the training targets (`encode_frame`, `spec.encode_targets`).

This cut is part of a larger body of work that is attempting to understand, clarify, and protect against "confounds" in our system, research, and training efforts.

Current state: Most of the initial design work is complete. As well as all of the scoped "pre-build" items so far.

- Design sketch for "walk infra" (see 7.23-1 below)
