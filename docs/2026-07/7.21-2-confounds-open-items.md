## Open review items

- **Verdict row 3 assumes native-vs-native measurement.** The contract-break cell of "New number vs old recorded number" ("comparable only if the input change didn't alter the information available") holds when each era's number was measured in-contract. By the doc's own vintage definition, a number recorded post-boundary *of an old checkpoint* is also a "new number", and for that mixed case the cell's verdict is wrong (it is the confounded case from rows 1 and 2). One clause pinning the cell to native measurements would close it.
- **Incident 1's mechanics-change rationale covers two of its three commits.** "The leaks made the earlier game easier" fits `2e6b2df` and `e04cad6`, but `5a2beb0` (army_delta) subtracts expected production, a quantity derivable from other channels, so it is a re-encoding (the doc's no-information-change case), not a leak. The incident-level union classification still stands.
- **Contract-break bullet wording candidate.** "Old checkpoints run under inputs, computation, or output decoding they never trained with" can be read as covering distribution shift, which is exactly the misreading the function-vs-distribution line (scratchpad §1) exists to prevent. Candidate: "run under input construction, computation, or output decoding that differs from training."

## Deferred extensions

- **Old checkpoint as data generator.** PPO self-play or distillation would use a checkpoint as a producer of training data, a seventh artifact use the six activities do not cover. Deferred until PPO is real; the doc's closing extension hook is the designated response. Related: under PPO, selection acquires a training-side counterpart (scratchpad §2's PPO caveat).
- **Inference-only forward changes.** Quantization or non-exact compilation would be a contract-break with no recipe-change, a profile no table row produces. Known non-case for this project today (also recorded in scratchpad §3).
