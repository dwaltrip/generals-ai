## Next action: the alive_mask decoupling refactor

The scope, as decided:

- alive_mask emission is gated by an explicit `emit_alive_mask` flag, threaded through the dataset constructor and `encode_frame`. Today the emission gate is the existence of the elim ctx, which ties a measurement choice to the elim internals. After the refactor, the ctx is built when the flag or a variant requires it, and the flag alone gates emission.
- Call sites that pass a variant now pass the flag explicitly (roughly 6 to 8 sites). The constructor validates that the time_bin variant comes with the mask enabled (its loss consumes it).
- Every occupied keyset is unchanged, so the refactor is verified byte-for-byte with the compare scripts from the 8.05 refactor (`tmp/dataset-refactor-byte-compare/`).
- The next_death dump hook forwards alive_mask for offline analysis. That stays. Its "the dataset emits it unconditionally" comment gets updated to cite the config.
- This should land before the goldens freeze the keysets.

## Working style notes

The session settled into a style that felt right, recorded here to continue it.

- **Discussion before code, one piece at a time.** Each piece gets its shape agreed in chat before anything is written. Judgment calls are surfaced rather than resolved silently, and the pace is deliberately slower than default.
- **Ease into new areas.** Orient with targeted reads of small files, and use sub-agent surveys for heavy code context instead of bulk reading. Surveys launch with explicit signoff and write on-disk notes under `tmp/2026-08-goldens-1st-cut-notes/`. The session's survey answered the TargetsConfig factoring question and several questions we had not thought to ask yet. The pattern paid off well.
- **Skeletons before implementations.** The goldens interfaces are sketched as real files in their real locations: typed signatures, `TODO(sketch)` comments for the main behavior, no implementations, no verbose docstrings. The sketches double as the synthesis of the design docs, and guesses are fine when they are visible as guesses.
- **Tier by clarity.** Start with the pieces that are settled and hard to get wrong, then their consumers, and hold back what is genuinely ambiguous (the regen internals, the report writer).
- **Pull warm threads, with scope checks.** The alive_mask trace grew into the emit semantics, the decoupling scope, and the measurement-config note, and each expansion was worth it. The counterweight also applied: enthusiasm for a larger refactor (the TrainConfig restructure) was converted into a parked scoping step instead of immediate work.
- **Prose register.** The session put sustained effort into plain, de-slopified writing in both code comments and docs. The drafting process used for 8.15-2 (scratch notes, plainify passes, then the doc) is reusable for future substantial notes.
