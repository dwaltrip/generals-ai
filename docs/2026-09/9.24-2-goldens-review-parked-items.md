# Goldens review: parked items

Date: 2026-09-24

**Daniel's level of review / sign-off for this note:** not yet reviewed. The items come from Claude's review of the goldens package, and most were not discussed in session.

The goldens review (9.23-1 and this session) led to the `KeyGroup` change and the refactor design in 9.24-1. This note lists the smaller items it found that neither covers. Two parked items are recorded in 9.24-1 instead: splitting `RefId` by surface (under "Deferred"), and the code TODO about content-addressed storage (under "Small items").

## Interfaces

- **Move `RefForm` and `stored_form` out of the registry.** `compare.py` imports the registry only to get `RefForm` and `KeyRef`. Moving `RefForm` and `stored_form` into `hashes.py` or a small `forms.py` would leave `compare`, `hashes`, `store`, and `render` free of project imports. This is a cheap step toward separating the generic machinery from project-specific code. `compare_supervision` would then take a mapping from key to `RefForm` instead of the `KeyRef` mapping.
- **Parameter names in `compare_supervision(raw, ref, refs)`.** `ref` is the dict of loaded arrays and `refs` is the `KeyRef` mapping, which read as the same thing. Names like `got`, `stored`, and `forms` would be clearer. This fits naturally with the item above.
- **Typed records for the abort messages.** The keyset and contradiction aborts are built as strings where they are raised. 9.19-1 made the warnings into records so that their wording belongs to the renderer, and the aborts could follow the same pattern. After the 9.24-1 refactor, they would live on the compute side.

## Store and loaders

- **`parse_rel_path` as the exact inverse of `rel_path`.** `rel_path` works on relative paths, while `parse_ref_path` takes an absolute path plus the root, and `build_ref_set` computes the relative path twice. A `parse_rel_path(rel)` would pair with `rel_path`, and the round-trip test could test that pair directly. It could also reject empty parts: `supervision/<fx>/@key.npy` currently parses to a `RefId` with an empty point.
- **Loader asymmetry in the golden tests.** The obs test loads with `store.load_obs`, while the supervision test goes through `loaders.load_supervision_reference`, a thin loop over `store.load_supervision`. That loop could move to `store`, leaving `loaders` with only `load_fixture`.

## Tests

- **Re-cut `tests/goldens` and `tests/goldens_lib`.** A proposed criterion: a test belongs in `goldens_lib` if it runs on synthetic input and doesn't read the project's registry data, fixtures, references, or revisions. By that rule:
  - `test_compare.py` fits `goldens_lib`, and so will `test_plan.py` after 9.24-1.
  - `test_store.py` is mixed. The round trip runs over every registry ref, which is project-side, while the off-scheme test is synthetic.
  - `test_regen.py` uses the real registry and producers, so it is project-side as written.
  - `test_mechanism_golden.py` is lib-side by this rule, but its role in the revisions workflow (a failure means a `mechanism-change` revision) could argue for leaving it where it is.
  - The chunk 4 blocking test will be project-side.

## Report

- **A removed fixture reads oddly for obs.** If a fixture is removed from the registry, each obs point prints two lines, `<point> unchanged` and `<point> removed (1 fixture)`. The second reads as if the point itself was removed. This will first show up at fixture selection.
- **`render_warning` is public** while every other function in `report.py` is private, and nothing else imports it.
