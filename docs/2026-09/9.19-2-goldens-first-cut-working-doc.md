## Where things stand

The goldens guard two surfaces, obs and supervision, over three throwaway fixtures and a registry of stored configs. Both golden tests pass on the committed references. The regen tool has been rebuilt around a plan-then-write structure, with a store module holding the tree layout and a report that says which references changed and where. The revisions directory, its record format, and the blocking test are designed (9.18-1) and not yet built.

Branch: `goldens-1st-cut-build`.
