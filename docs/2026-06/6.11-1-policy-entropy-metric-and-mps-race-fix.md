# Session summary — policy-entropy metric, bc/eval package, MPS H2D race fix

Date: 2026-06-11

## What landed

- **Policy entropy as a standing val metric** (6.10-6 follow-up #1): mean H in nats (non-pass frames) computed during `run_val`, written to `epochs.jsonl` under `val.policy_entropy`, rendered as optional `v_H` / `v_eH` (= e^H) columns in `compare_runs` and `quality.md`, and printed on the per-epoch console line. One stored number; e^H is derived at the report layer.
- **`bc/eval/` package**: `eval.py` split into `run.py` (val-pass orchestrator) and `metrics.py` (diagnostic meters — `PolicyEntropyMeter`, `ActionDistMeter`), facade `__init__` keeps the `training.bc.eval` import path. Pattern intended to match the planned `bc/model/` split: facade `__init__`, focused internal modules.
- **MPS H2D race root-caused and fixed.** The long-standing local val crash (`TODO(mps-val-crash)`) was never the bool-mask gather it was attributed to: `move_batch`'s `non_blocking=True` is genuinely honored on MPS, and the call sites rebind the batch dict, freeing the CPU source tensors while the async copy can still be in flight. The use-after-free corrupted batch tensors (sometimes crashing, sometimes completing silently with garbage metrics). Fixed via `is_non_blocking_safe_device` in `training/shared/device.py` — async copies CUDA-only. Full story in that docstring; symptom catalog in the commit message. Local MPS val now runs with sane, bit-identical-across-runs metrics.

## Old local-MPS numbers — assessed risk: low

The race corrupts silently, so anything that used `move_batch` on MPS pre-fix is exposed in principle: the train loop (local smoke runs only — all load-bearing training is cloud/CUDA, unaffected), and the six saved stratified dumps behind the 6.10 analyses. For the dumps the recorded evidence points to clean: 6.10-4 logged the dump-time cross-checks against cloud-computed `epochs.jsonl` (full-frac value CE agrees to ≤5e-5, frame counts exact; frac-0.5 agrees to 4–8e-3, consistent with subsampling), and every observed instance of the corruption was gross (values off by orders of magnitude, impossible counts), not subtle drift. Caveats: "always gross" is an empirical characterization from one debugging session, and the frac-0.5 check is statistical. Definitive check if ever wanted: re-run a dump with fixed code (same seed → identical frames) and diff per-frame arrays against the saved `.npz`, ~5 min per dump; the two frac-0.5 `runs-cloud` dumps are the ones to prioritize.
