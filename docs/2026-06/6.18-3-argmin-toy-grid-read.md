# Argmin toy: the grid read, and where it points next

*Date: 2026-06-18. Status: session note. The full 16-cell grid ran on `cloud_24k_sub_5k` and confirmed the encoding-ladder / notch predictions — and showed the argmin readout is cleanly learnable in a faithful representation, which is evidence it is not the aux-head bottleneck. Records the grid read, the early-stop + reporting infra added, and a check of the prod obs against the design: it confirms 6.17-1 §3.1 (prod hands the model the per-player total as a broadcast constant) and answers §9's open question about the aux head's input — which points the bottleneck at the trunk preserving a provided signal, not spatial aggregation. Surrender re-admission is the chosen next direction — it revives the encoding question by making army value no longer a sufficient statistic. Builds on 6.18-1 (plan), 6.18-2 (anchor), and 6.17-1 §3.1/§9 (scope).*

## What ran

The full first-slice grid from 6.18-1 §7, on `cloud_24k_sub_5k` (val split, 250 games, 105,538 frames post-surrender-filter), `lr 0.1`, 300 epochs — **16 cells, 24 runs** (the 4 notch × depth-1 cells run 3 seeds each per Risk #3; everything else 1 seed). The "16 vs 24" reconciles as: 16 cells = 4 all-alive (1 encoding × 4 models) + 12 mixed (3 encodings × 4 models); the seed expansion on the 4 notch cells adds 8 runs → 24.

## What it showed — the encoding ladder bites as designed

`acc_strict` (the load-bearing measure — full-set restricted to non-tied frames), reading down the encoding axis on the **mixed** population:

| encoding | A1 (linear) | D1 (equiv-linear) | B1 (depth-1) | E1_mlp (depth-1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| explicit_mask | 0.84 | **1.00** | 0.89 | **1.00** (E1_linear) |
| zero_overload | 0.38 | **0.00** | ~0.90 | **1.00** |
| neg1_sentinel | 0.36 | **0.00** | ~0.89 | **1.00** |

(all-alive, encodings collapse: A1 0.96, B1 0.90, D1 1.00, E1_linear 1.00.)

- **`explicit_mask` is linear-solvable** — the equivariant linear models (D1, E1_linear) reach 1.0, and D1's weights show the *predicted* solution: `A = [−166 (army), +1028 (alive)]` ≈ `−army + BIG·alive`. Dead sit at 0, alive get +1028, smallest-army-alive wins.
- **`zero_overload` / `neg1_sentinel` are notches** — linear models (A1, D1) fail, depth-1 models (B1, E1_mlp) succeed, across both notch encodings and all 3 seeds.
- **The sharpest cell is `D1 / zero_overload = 0.000`.** D1 is a clean equivariant comparator (`score_p = A·army_p + B·Σarmy`); to prefer small army needs `A<0`, but then dead-at-0 scores highest and it picks a dead player on *every* mixed frame. Its learned weights confirm the collapse: `A = +0.47` — the wrong sign, and near zero. A model architecturally constrained to a clean linear comparator therefore fails on every mixed frame: the notch at its sharpest.

The weights, not just the accuracies, recovered the design's predicted structures — stronger evidence than accuracy alone, since the predicted structure is visible directly in the parameters.

## Secondary findings (mechanistic, well-supported)

- **The tie gap appears only in the asymmetric models.** `acc_full` vs `acc_strict`: A1 all-alive shows the ~4-pt gap from the lowest-index tie convention; D1/E1 show **none** (`acc_full == acc_strict`) despite the same 60%-tie population. Reason: on an exact army tie the equivariant models emit *exactly equal* logits for the tied players → `argmax`'s lowest-index tiebreak coincides with the `label` convention for free. The metric gap is itself a readout of whether the model is symmetric.
- **Inductive bias is double-edged.** The equivariance prior *helps* where a linear solution exists (D1/E1 = 1.0, beating A1's 0.84–0.96) and *hurts* on the notch (D1 = 0.00 vs A1's 0.38 — A1's unconstrained 8×8 finds a partial asymmetric shortcut the 2-param D1 can't access).
- **acc and loss decouple in the tiny models.** D1/E1 hit acc 1.0 on all-alive but val_loss stays 1.12: with 1–2 params they rank perfectly but have no scale knob to sharpen CE (they inflate their comparator weight to ~−1347 fruitlessly over 300 epochs). This is the mechanism behind "equivariant cells hit 1.0 fast, then waste epochs" — the early-stop motivation.
- **`neg1_sentinel` is easier to optimize than `zero_overload`.** Both are notches, but dead-at−1 is linearly separable from all live (≥0), so the ReLU gate has margin; dead-at-0 sits adjacent to small-army-alive (~0.001), a razor-thin band. E1_mlp converges by ~epoch 1 (neg1) vs ~5–7 (zero_overload), final loss 0.010 vs 0.030. Separability eases the carve — but only for the *depth-1* models; D1 is still 0.000 on neg1 (a linear model can't draw any notch).
- **B1 is the unstable/overfitting one.** Its val_loss diverges late (explicit_mask 0.19 train / 1.55 val, min 0.37) while accuracy holds — distinct from the A1/D1 *representational floor* (train and val both high). Independent argument for best-checkpoint visibility, which the new reporting now provides.

## The instrumental conclusion (hypothesis, supported)

**In a faithful representation, the argmin-with-exclusion readout is cleanly learnable** — linearly on `explicit_mask`, depth-1 on the notch. Combined with 6.15-2 (the trunk preserves army at ~0.82 R²), this points the aux-head gap **upstream** — to army fidelity / ranking under noise — **not the argmin readout**. Two riders are now moot: `gap_scale` (depth-1 didn't fail on the notch, so the rescaled-gap control isn't needed) and Risk #3 (3 seeds tight — no unlucky-seed false negative).

Stated as a hypothesis the evidence supports, not proof — and sharpened below: the toy tested argmin over a clean *vector*, which is not the broadcast→conv path prod's trunk takes.

## Infra landed (supporting)

Early-stop + reporting, in the `argmin_toy` package only (prod `bc/train.py` untouched, verified by usage grep):

- **Early stop** on val_loss plateau (`patience=30 / min_delta=1e-3 / min_epochs=20`); the loop returns `TrainResult(curves, best_epoch, stopped_epoch)` and **keeps the final weights** (rather than restoring best), so best + last both stay reportable — B1's late divergence is itself signal.
- **`report.py`** → auto-writes `report.md` beside `results.json` each run, via `utils.format.md_table`: per-run table with last + best loss, the **train-val gap**, best/stop epochs, and the two accuracies.
- The grid science is from the full 300-epoch run; early-stop is for *future* runs, not a re-read.

## Checking the prod obs against the design (6.17-1 §3.1 / §9)

This isn't a new direction — 6.17-1 already scoped it. §3.1 ("the ecological format") states prod presents `total_army_N` as a **broadcast constant** across the board, so the toy's task is "argmin-of-a-vector, **not** spatial summation"; the toy uses that format deliberately and "**bypass[es] spatial aggregation entirely**" (§9), with raw-tile aggregation named as the *second* set of experiments. What this session adds is verifying §3.1 against the prod code and answering one of §9's own open questions — *"what the aux head's **input** actually is (raw tiles vs something pooled)."*

- **Verified in `bc/obs.py`:** the per-player total is the broadcast channel group `self_army_count` / `opp_1..7_army_count` — `(armies * owned_mask).sum()` over the *whole* board (`scoreboard_row` / `init_memory`), `/1000` (`_ARMY_DIVISOR`), **fog-inclusive** (the public scoreboard, which the project already treats as obs input). Same quantity and units as the toy's `ARMY_SIM`. So §9's open input question resolves as **both**: the aux head is handed the pooled total *and* the raw tiles (`army_magnitude` + owner masks) — it is **not forced to aggregate from tiles** to get the army total.
- **Reuse is nearly free** if we go there: the fq machinery already materializes the obs tensor, an **`ARMY_OBS` deriver already exists** (`families/army_derivers.py`, beside `ARMY_SIM`), and `army_obs == army_sim` parity is established (modulo fp16).
- **The current toy is the vector layout**, not the broadcast one (`encode()` returns `[B, 8, C]`; `broadcast` `[B, 8, C, H, W]` is `NotImplementedError`). Per §3.1/§5 these are equivalent for the pooled/vector families — pooling a constant channel is lossless, and a single conv over a constant field collapses to a scaled 1×1. So nothing in the architecture *forces* the trunk to lose a broadcast-constant army channel; a conv can carry it as a scaled constant.
- **The puzzle that surfaces:** 6.15-2 still measures the trunk preserving army at only ~0.82 R². Since §5 says a conv *can* carry a constant channel losslessly, the shortfall from R²≈1 is not an architectural necessity — it points to a training / channel-mixing / capacity effect, and pinning down which is the real open question. It also refines §9's premise: §9 anticipated the harder contest as "raw-tile aggregation is hard to learn," but the army *total* isn't aggregated-from-tiles in prod — it's a constant the trunk is handed and partly loses.

**Net (hypothesis):** the aux-head gap is neither the readout (toy: cleanly learnable) nor aggregation-from-tiles (prod hands over the pooled total) but **the trunk attenuating an army signal it's given** — a loss §5 says the architecture does not force. Assembled from the toy + 6.15-2 + the verified obs reading; not tested end-to-end.

## Next

**Superseded (6/18):** forward direction for this line now lives in [6.18-9](6.18-9-elim-head-synthesis-and-direction.md).

Two distinct faithfulness gaps between the toy and prod came into focus this session, and they motivate different threads:

**1. Surrender re-admission (chosen next direction) — revives the encoding question.** The MVP *filters* surrender frames (`~alive & army>0`) to restore the clean binary `alive ≡ army>0`, which is what makes the 3-rung encoding ladder faithful and gives the clean notch result above. But that filter also removes the hardest part of the encoding question. A surrendered player is a *third status*: the elim mask reads dead, yet they hold real army on the board. On that population:

- **Army value alone stops being a sufficient statistic.** A surrendered player at army 5 (out) looks identical to a live player at army 5 (in). So the `none` status-encoding (army-only) has an *irreducible* ceiling — not a notch the right architecture can carve, but genuinely missing information.
- **The encoding choice becomes load-bearing for correctness, not just optimization difficulty.** To exclude the right players the encoding must *carry status* — boolean `alive`, or a 3-way categorical (alive / surrendered / eliminated, cheaply derivable from `(alive, army>0)`). The status-encoding axis {none / boolean / categorical} (6.18-1 §12) becomes the headline axis, where on the filtered MVP it was degenerate.
- **There's a label-semantics fork too:** argmin over `alive` (elim-based) vs over `army>0` (on-board). These differ exactly on surrender frames; which is the right target depends on the downstream question. A one-line toggle, compared on the surrender population.

This is more faithful to prod (surrendered players genuinely hold army there) and re-elevates encoding from pedagogy to "determines whether the task is solvable from the given inputs." Where the obs check is about **army fidelity** (does the trunk preserve a value it's given), surrender is about **status sufficiency** (do the inputs even carry what's needed) — two separate gaps.

**2. The trunk thread (highest scientific signal, separate build).** Continue 6.15-2: *why* does the trunk lose army it's handed, when a conv *can* carry a constant channel losslessly (§5)? The `broadcast` layout rung is the bridge — it would exercise the broadcast→conv path inside the toy's controlled setting, the missing rung between "vector readout is easy" and "the prod conv degrades it." Not a clean toy; an architecture/probe study.

**Cheap confirmations available:** swap `ARMY_SIM → ARMY_OBS` (~one line) to confirm "obs army is clean enough that argmin stays trivial," closing the input-side question (low new signal but near-free). §9's literal raw-tile aggregation toy is *lower-value than it looks* for the army total — prod hands over the pooled total, so the trunk-preservation puzzle above is the better target (raw-tile processing would still matter for army *geometry*, which the scoreboard doesn't give).

The space is open; the lean is surrender next — well-scoped, prod-faithful, and the agreed next direction — with the trunk/broadcast thread as the higher-signal follow-on.
