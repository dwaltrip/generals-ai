## Soft / smoothed categorical targets for value learning

- **Farebrother et al., ICML 2024 — "Stop Regressing: Training Value Functions via Classification for Scalable Deep RL"** ([arXiv:2403.03950](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03950), [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/farebrother24a.html)). Closest published analog to our design question. Value functions trained as categorical classification; the winning target construction across Atari, robotic manipulation, chess-without-search, and Wordle is **HL-Gauss** — target mass spread across neighboring bins (Gaussian kernel), exploiting ordinal bin structure. Consistently beat MSE regression and sharper categorical targets (two-hot). Gains attributed to robustness against noisy, non-stationary targets — relevant to us because a per-game fixed placement label is an extremely noisy per-frame target. Also reported robustness across smoothing widths, supporting "pick a sensible τ, don't sweep it." (Verified via abstract/search 2026-06-10; full read pending.)
- **Diaz & Marathe, CVPR 2019 — SORD (soft ordinal labels)**. Soft targets decaying with rank distance beat one-hot CE on age estimation, depth, aesthetics ranking. Our exp(−|Δrank|/τ) kernel is this family; HL-Gauss is the same idea with a Gaussian kernel.
- **Müller, Kornblith & Hinton, NeurIPS 2019 — "When does label smoothing help?"** The standard caution: smoothing improves generalization/calibration but erases learned inter-class similarity structure in representations (network outputs the imposed kernel shape instead). Mild cost for us — placement ordinality is true structure we *want* imposed — but it's why "strictly better" overclaims in general.
- Adjacent family (not the cheap option, head redesign): unimodal output constraints (Beckham & Pal 2017), cumulative-link ordinal decompositions (CORAL/CORN).

## n-player projects and how they handled multi-player outcomes

The marquee results (AlphaGo/AlphaZero, AlphaStar, OpenAI Five) are all 2-player or 2-team — two-player zero-sum is where minimax = Nash and a scalar win-prob value suffices. The >2-sided projects each needed bespoke outcome machinery:

- **Suphx (Microsoft, 2020; 4-player riichi mahjong)** — the real placement precedent. Final outcome is 1st–4th over ~8–12 rounds with nonlinear placement payoffs. **Global Reward Prediction (GRP)**: a network trained on human logs to predict expected final game reward from the round-results trajectory so far; per-round RL reward is the *delta* in that prediction (potential-based shaping). Turns the sparse rank-shaped terminal payoff into a dense per-round signal; standings-dependence of correct play falls out of the conditioning. Two reads for us: (a) GRP is structurally our value head's intended PPO-era job; (b) GRP's input was essentially the scoreboard (interim scores), sufficient for round-granularity shaping — coarse standings carry real placement signal, and a scoreboard-only predictor is roughly what our conditional floors are built to beat. Its per-round-delta structure also gave it a per-state-varying target by construction — mild outside evidence for the "denser per-frame targets" lever in 6.10-1.
- **Pluribus (CMU/Facebook, Science 2019; 6-player no-limit hold'em)** — dodged placement: cash-game poker resolves each hand to a scalar chip delta, no ranking. Its n-player machinery went into search robustness (leaf nodes evaluate against several opponent continuation strategies, since no unique game value exists in 6-player). The transferable lesson is the framing: abandoned equilibrium semantics for the value function, optimized empirical winnings. Relevant at PPO time, not for BC target design.
- **Cicero (Meta, Science 2022; 7-player Diplomacy)** — full FFA dynamics (alliances, kingmaking) plus natural-language negotiation; preceded by no-press Diplomacy work (Meta, DeepMind ~2020–21).
- **GT Sophy (Sony, Nature 2022; GT racing)** — n-car races with ranked finish, though competitive interaction is local.
- **DouZero (2021; 3-player Doudizhu)** — dynamic team structure (landlord vs. peasants).
- Many-agent FFA *platforms* exist (Neural MMO, Melting Pot) but no flagship superhuman result on them.
