## The short version

The sweep resolved every preregistered fork, and the combined picture is consistent across cells: **trunk damage tracks the value gradient's magnitude and its memorization content; dropout placement and head capacity are not meaningful levers.** The single most consequential cell was lam0.1 — full policy recovery with a hard-CE, heavily-memorizing head, just by turning λ from 0.5 to 0.1 — which moves much of the explanation for τ's policy rescue from "removed the fingerprints" to "weakened the push." Meanwhile the stratified dumps show that every tamed head, regardless of config, knows the same shallow thing (survivor counting + bucket priors) with no within-bucket signal at this data scale. Do-no-harm is solved, by two independent routes; nothing in knob-space makes the head know more. The open questions have moved to data scale, target design, and the PPO hand-off.

## Fork resolutions, cell by cell

**lam0.1 → the magnitude branch (the headline).** ep3 val_policy 0.9636 — inside the anchor band, indistinguishable from λ=0 (0.9615) — while the head memorizes hard (gap@best 1.315, never below floor, train value CE diving to 0.38 by ep2). The fingerprint content still flows into the trunk at λ=0.1, one-fifth as strong, and the policy doesn't measurably care. Calibrated statement: a 5× λ reduction alone is *sufficient* for full policy recovery, so content removal is *not necessary* for it. What τ uniquely provides is no longer the policy rescue — it's the working head (lam0.1's head is useless on val; the τ cells' heads beat the floor). λ is promoted to a first-class lever.

**tau0.3 → containment breaks.** Gap@best 1.097, never beats floor, best epoch back at ep2 — and the worst ep3 policy of the sweep: 0.9761, which is (0.9761 − 0.964) / 0.005 ≈ 2.5σ above the anchor mean. Sharper targets restore the memorization payoff, and the trunk pays for it. τ=0.6 sits near the working edge; don't tune τ downward.

**tau1.0 → the strongest cell, with one open confound.** Smallest gap (0.495), best ep3 policy (0.9620, at the λ=0 ceiling), and below the global floor on hard CE (−0.061) *despite* paying the heaviest softness tax. Two caveats: the gap shrink is partly mechanical (the hard-CE value of fully memorizing the softened targets rises with τ, so the achievable gap compresses — ~0.3 at τ=0.6 per [`6.10-5`](6.10-5-value-head-synthesis.md), not computed for τ=1.0; its train value CE sat flat at ~0.85 with no memorization dive at all), and its stratified bucket reads are tax-confounded (below). Judged per its preregistration (gap / policy / stratified direction), it wins on the first two; the third is currently unmeasurable.

**prepre_drop, skip_drop → placement does not measurably matter.** Both land at the weak end of the anchor's Δbest range (−0.060 / −0.083) and the top of its gap band (0.756 / 0.730). They are statistically indistinguishable from each other and from the anchor; the nominal orderings even disagree across metrics (skip_drop ahead on every aggregate, prepre_drop ahead on the late-game stratified reads — all inside single-seed noise; the strongest single number is prepre's 2-alive Δ of −0.062, which is ~1.9σ below its conditional floor against the bucket SE of 0.033, and the prepre-vs-skip *difference* there is only ~1.2σ once both buckets' SEs are propagated). The mechanistically-motivated placements did not beat the naive one.

**direct-drop, direct-nodrop → capacity is not the lever either.** Viable but uniformly slightly worse: barely below floor (−0.029 / −0.021), gaps at or above the anchor band (0.780 / 0.823), ep3 policy at or above the band (0.9707 / 0.9750). The capacity-sets-the-residual-gap prediction is **not supported** — the 9k-param head memorizes as much as the 0.82M pyramid. Thin-head-for-PPO remains possible (it beats the floor and expresses survivor counting fine) but offers no advantage. Dropout-vs-none at direct: differences of 0.004–0.05, same weak-positive direction as the old ~1.8σ pyramid read, still unresolved.

## A candidate damage law (hypothesis, well-organized by the data)

Reading trunk health as matched-epoch val_policy: among the six λ=0.5 cells, **policy ordering tracks memorization-gap ordering exactly, 6 for 6** (tau1.0 0.50 → 0.9620; skip_drop 0.73 → 0.9675; prepre_drop 0.76 → 0.9694; direct-drop 0.78 → 0.9707; direct-nodrop 0.82 → 0.9750; tau0.3 1.10 → 0.9761). Adjacent pairs are individually inside seed noise (σ≈0.005 against a total spread of 0.014), so the claim is the rank agreement, not any pairwise delta. lam0.1 is the off-curve point in exactly the direction a *content × magnitude* law predicts: worst-tier gap (1.315), healthy-tier policy, because λ scales the whole relationship down.

A "recovery" rescaling makes the tiers readable (this is presentation, not a script output): `recovered = (1.039 − ep3 v_pol) / (1.039 − 0.9615)`, i.e., each cell's position on the 0.0775-nat line from the untamed baseline (0%) to the λ=0 ceiling (100%). Every tested config recovers ≥~80% of the damage; the tamed family spans ~80–100%. Significance limits: a difference between two cells' percentages carries σ ≈ 0.005·√2 / 0.0775 ≈ ±9pp (so the tiers are real, the fine distinctions are not), both anchors are single runs with their own ±0.005, and the 100% ceiling assumes the value gradient can only hurt — the assumption the aux-head direction is designed to break.

This law is a one-seed-per-cell pattern. It is strong enough to guide the next configuration choice, not strong enough to state as established.

## The stratified layer: all tamed heads know the same thing

First full use of the per-epoch in-training dumps (full-frac, 112,975 frames / 250 perspectives per epoch). Findings:

- **No within-bucket signal at sub_5k, in any cell.** Late-game buckets (2-/3-alive) sit at their conditional floors across skip_drop, prepre_drop, direct-drop, tau1.0 (Δ −0.06 to +0.10 against SEs of 0.03–0.06). The global below-floor headlines come almost entirely from survivor-counting, which the conditional floors give away. The 6.10-1 win condition — below conditional floor late-game — remains unmet at sub_5k by every configuration tried to date; the only glimpse of within-bucket signal remains the 24k bonus run's ep2 (~2σ). **The discriminative-signal question now looks like a data-scale question, not a config question.**
- **Survivor-counting is intact everywhere** — prediction entropy slides correctly with players-alive in every cell, including direct at 9k params. No collapse anywhere in the sweep.
- **lam0.1's head is the textbook fingerprint memorizer**: much sharper predictions than any other cell (overall pred-H 0.81 vs ~1.2–1.4) and confidently wrong on val in every undecided bucket (Δ +0.30 to +0.65) — the worst failure mode, sitting under one of the best policies. Reinforces the magnitude conclusion.
- **The skip-route hypothesis failed its train-side half.** skip_drop kept the global signal (predicted) but its memorization didn't shrink (gap in the anchor band) — the fingerprints don't need the full-resolution skip route, or don't live in the pre-module at all. Converges with the prepre null and lam0.1: where the head-side dropout sits was not the operative variable.
- **tau1.0's bucket reads are tax-confounded** — its late-game Δs (+0.10, +0.015) are within the τ=1.0 softness tax, so real within-bucket signal could be hiding under the handicap. The 1-alive bucket isolates the tax and works as a consistency check: it is all 1st-place outcomes (conditional floor 0.0), so a fully-certain τ-trained head emits the kernel's edge row and pays exactly `−ln(true-class mass)` in hard CE — `−ln(1/Σₖ₌₀..₇ e^(−k/τ))` ≈ 0.21 nats at τ=0.6 and 0.46 at τ=1.0. Observed: lam0.1 +0.11 (no tax, hard CE), τ0.6 cells +0.30–0.37, tau1.0 +0.47 ≈ the at-certainty tax. **Tooling gap: a tax-adjusted conditional floor** (CE of emitting the softened target under the bucket's truth distribution) would make τ levels comparable in the stratified report. Small, worth building before any τ decision.
- **Erosion is a regime property.** Every cell's value optimum lands at ep2–3 of 5 with decay after — the 5-epoch extension successfully un-censored the optimum (the old 3-epoch cells were peaking at ep3-of-3, unreadable). tau1.0's ep3→ep5 shows the same uniform retreat-toward-priors signature as the 6.10 24k bonus run (every bucket's Δ worsens, pred-H rises, argmax mode share 65%→40%). No config meets the optimum-at-final-epoch criterion; best-value and best-policy checkpoints diverge everywhere.

## What this closes, what it opens

Closed (at sub_5k scale, single seeds, with the stated caveats):

- Do-no-harm: solved by two independent routes (τ contains content; low λ caps magnitude). The [`6.09-1`](6.09-1-value-head-direction-and-head-metrics.md) BC-phase bar for the value head is met.
- Dropout placement: not a lever. Head capacity: not a lever for the residual gap. τ direction: down is harmful, 0.6–1.0 is the working range, 1.0 possibly best pending the tax-adjusted read.

Opened or relocated:

- **Scale**: does within-bucket placement signal emerge at 24k+? (Only answerable there.)
- **Targets**: denser per-frame signals (aux-head menu) are now the only untried lever aimed at making the head's signal *large* rather than safe — this sweep strengthened that rationale considerably.
- **PPO hand-off**: the trunk-vs-readout probe is unchanged in design, cheaper to justify now (it's pure verification — nothing left to fix first), and also decides whether the universal erosion even matters.
- **Strength**: the val_policy→win-rate exchange rate remains uncalibrated; all of this sweep's effects are loss-space effects.

## Direction decision + closure items

Decision (this session): **step back from regularization-knob tuning** — the knobs are characterized and the head's knowledge has plateaued at this scale; further cells in this space have low expected information. The strategic fork to settle fresh: aux heads (attack the signal-starvation directly) vs. 24k-scale work (real BC run, dense-history rematch, within-bucket question) vs. PPO bring-up (the stated blocker is lifted) vs. eval/strength calibration. Not mutually exclusive; the lit pass (Suphx GRP / DeepNash value head / Farebrother — scoped in [`6.10-2`](6.10-2-value-target-literature-pointers.md), still unlaunched) and the trunk probe are cheap and feed all of them.

Closure items for the knob chapter, neither yet done:

1. **τ0.6 + λ0.1 combo cell** — one run, not exploration: a config-of-record decision. The damage law predicts it lands at the λ=0 ceiling *with* a working (below-floor) head — the recipe future runs would inherit. Confirm rather than assume.
2. **Tax-adjusted conditional floors** in the stratified report (small), so τ=1.0 vs τ=0.6 head quality is readable before picking the default τ.
