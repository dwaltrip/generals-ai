## Build approach

Path 1, per §7 of [6.27-1](6.27-1-checkpoint-config-chunk-1-design.md): reshape directly at the writer-flip. Step 1, the strangler seam, is the natural first slice.

## Working style

- **Interface-first.** Slot the seams and types — `load_checkpoint`, `resolve_checkpoint_config`, `build_model`, `ConfiguredModel` — into place incrementally, rather than landing the whole pipeline at once.
- **Slow and collaborative.** Agent + Daniel, discussing as we go, not barreling through bundles.
