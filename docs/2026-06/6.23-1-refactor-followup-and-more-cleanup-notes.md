## Deferred robustness thread

- **`model_out` / dict typing.** The `dict[str, torch.Tensor]` pattern (`model_out`, `targets`, the loss return) carries no key or shape safety — `model_out` is the most flagrant. Two separable concerns: **keys** (TypedDict on the fixed-schema dicts — pyright-only, zero runtime; leave the dynamic aux keys open) and **shape** (boundary asserts at `bc_loss` / dataset emit, or jaxtyping if it recurs). Scoped first cut: TypedDict on `model_out` core + `targets` plus a couple of boundary shape asserts; pilot on one boundary to see the ergonomics before going wider. Higher value than the tidy-ups; a fresh thread, not a continuation.

## Regular priority

- **`loss.py` → folder.** Re-measure size first. The 6.21-1 lean was a *partial vertical* split (`config.py` / `core.py` / `accumulator.py`); the head `nn.Module`s stay in `model/heads/`. Decide on the post-refactor size.

## Decision-gated (want a discussion pass first, not a "just code" session)

- **Loss config → `.pt` checkpoint** (with a `LEGACY_LOSS` pin mirroring `LEGACY_ARCH`). The objective is the one resume-critical piece with no per-checkpoint pin — it lives only in `args.json` and leans on "default == historical behavior." Slice 4 made it nearly free (`config.loss` is one object to serialize). It's a checkpoint-format change with a back-compat tail — its own yes/no call.

- **Slice 3 — naming cleanup.** Two scopes: surgical (`next_death_target → next_removal_target`, no serialized strings touched) vs. full `elim_`/`next_elim_` rename that reaches checkpoint/dump/config strings and needs the `dump_compat` aliasing pattern. Settle scope before picking a scheme.
