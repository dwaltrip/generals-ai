# Argmin toy: vertical slice built, anchor greened

*Date: 2026-06-18. Status: session note. The argmin-toy implementation (6.18-1 plan) is built end-to-end and the A1 / all-alive sanity anchor recovers `W ≈ −I`. Records the build state, the anchor result, and the probe_500-overfit finding that reshapes where development happens. The grid is next.*

## The anchor result — A1 / all-alive recovers `W ≈ −I`

The sanity cell (army-only, linear A1) greens on `cloud_24k_sub_5k` but **not** on `probe_500`. The contrast localizes the cause to data scale:

| | probe_500 (33 games) | cloud_24k_sub_5k (250 games) | synthetic IID |
|---|---|---|---|
| val acc_strict | 0.64 (plateaued) | **0.966** | 0.975 |
| train / val loss | 0.35 / 0.71 | 0.26 / 0.24 | matched |
| sym_subspace_residual | 0.36 | 0.13 | 0.027 |
| `W` structure | uniform ≈ −5.5, no diagonal | diagonal-dominant (`row0 = [−660, +47, …]`) | clean `a·I+b·11ᵀ` |

On cloud the diagonal entry is strongly negative and off-diagonals are small positive (the `11ᵀ` common-army term, which doesn't affect argmax); errors concentrate at margin < 2 and are ~0 for margin ≥ 2 — the sharp comparator the design predicts.

The decisive signal is the **train/val gap**: present on probe_500 (0.35 vs 0.71), gone on cloud (0.26 vs 0.24). The machinery was already proven correct on synthetic IID armies (clean `−I`, no gap), so probe_500's failure is **overfitting on 33 correlated games** — exactly 6.18-1 Risk #8. The argmin step is cleanly learnable by a linear readout in the clean representation: **it is not the aux-head bottleneck in that representation** (the instrumental precondition of 6.17-1 §9).

## Two findings that change the plan

- **probe_500 is too small for the all-alive cell.** 33 games → highly-correlated early-game frames → the linear oracle overfits slot/game shortcuts rather than learning `−I`. The doc's "develop on probe_500, final read on cloud_24k_sub_5k" (§8) doesn't hold for the all-alive regime — that cell needs cloud data to develop on at all, not just for the final read. The mixed cells (more games-spread) may tolerate probe_500, but the safe default is to run the whole grid on `cloud_24k_sub_5k`.
- **all-alive is a 60%-tie regime.** `exact_tie_frac = 0.605` on the all-alive population (vs the ~12.7% global estimate) — the early-game "everyone near army 1" opening. acc_full sits ~4 pts below acc_strict purely from ties. acc_strict / acc_inset are the load-bearing measures here even more than 6.18-1 §6 anticipated; acc_full is not a failure signal in this regime.
