## 2. The seed replicates: the session's empirical result

Two seed replicates of the 6.10 `tau0.6-drop-n20` sub_5k cell (run in a parallel session) gave the noise yardstick everything now reads against: **policy-side σ ≈ 0.005 nats** (the 0.02–0.03-nat findings of the 6.10 arc survive; sub-0.01 orderings are below the noise floor), **value-side much noisier** (best-epoch val_value range 0.073, final-epoch range 0.21, trajectory shapes differ — one seed peaked at epoch 1 and degraded after). The σ is an n=3 estimate and could easily be 2× off in either direction.

The raw triple, since this note is its durable record — final-epoch val_policy 0.9646 / 0.9685 / 0.9591; best-epoch val_value 1.2628 / 1.2979 / 1.3362 against floor 1.406 (Δbest −0.143 / −0.108 / −0.070); gap@best 0.63–0.74. This is the reference distribution Wave A reads against.

Consequences for existing claims: "below floor" is seed-robust (all three seeds land below), while "dropout stacks on τ" dropped to a ~1.8σ read and the scale-invariant-gap observation weakened. The practical consequences are that value-sensitive comparisons need coarse forks or replicates, and value-head checkpoint selection has to be per-run, not per-config.

## 3. Design discussions that shaped Wave A

- **Dropout theory and the two-route picture.** The placement question got sharper once we looked inside the head's pyramid: it's tiny (initial conv + two down + two up blocks), and its skips form a shallow full-resolution detail route around the 8×8 bottleneck — a plausible fingerprint conduit, since fingerprints are exactly high-resolution spatially-exact information while placement signal should be global and compressed. That produced the two placement hypotheses now running (before-`pre`, skip-site), both explicitly *unverified mechanism stories* — including the premise that the residual memorization lives in this module at all. If both placement cells fail to move the gap, that's converging evidence the fingerprints live upstream, redirecting us to λ and weight decay.
- **Harness audit.** Kept sub_5k (stratified power is already marginal at 5k; the new noise yardstick is bound to it; it has a demonstrated transfer record to 24k). Extended 3→5 epochs to un-censor the value optimum — safe because the LR is constant (no scheduler; the only ramp is the legacy-resume warmup, a no-op on fresh runs), so epochs 1–3 stay matched to all 3-epoch history.
- **Seeds.** Single-seed first pass, targeted replicates where reads land ambiguous, with the 3-seed anchor triple as the reference distribution. Fits the 10-concurrent-GPU cap with headroom.
- Explicitly deferred: p-value tuning, the pre-Linear-dropout ablation, skip-*removal* (the dropout version was chosen instead), bottleneck dropout (argued against — it taxes the route the head should rely on), a bigger manifest, and the direct+drop-no-τ corner.

### Interpretation notes

**direct-drop / direct-nodrop.** The `direct` head was historically ruled out — it memorized with gaps of 1.15–1.9 — but every one of those runs used hard CE targets, so the verdict was confounded by the two failure modes. With τ containing memorization, the capacity question is finally clean. Three things to look at. First, does it still beat the floor? If yes, the real signal (survivor counting, board context) doesn't need head capacity to express — which supports the "thin head reading a rich trunk" shape we'd want for PPO, where the critic's capacity lives in the shared trunk rather than the head. Second, where does the train−val gap land? The anchor's band is 0.63–0.74; if direct lands clearly below that, it suggests head capacity is what sets the leftover memorization (less room to store fingerprints), making head size a real lever. Third, watch for collapse: if the head goes constant (check prediction entropy in the dumps, not just the loss — a constant head can sit innocently at the floor), then ~9k params is too little to even express the signal. The drop-vs-nodrop pair also re-asks "does dropout add anything on top of τ" — a question that was only a ~1.8σ read at pyramid — in a different capacity regime. On the policy side, val_policy should sit inside the anchor band (0.959–0.969) if head capacity is policy-neutral.

**lam0.1.** This is the attribution experiment. τ did two things at once: it removed the *payoff* for memorizing (a softened label can't be driven to zero loss) and it shrank the value gradient's *magnitude* (softer targets, smaller errors). The 6.10 policy recovery can't tell which one mattered. This cell separates them: hard CE targets (full fingerprint incentive intact) but λ turned down 5×, so the gradient is weak. If the policy recovers to ~0.96 while the head still memorizes hard (gap > 1), then what hurts the trunk is simply how hard the value loss pushes, whatever it's pushing toward — λ becomes a primary lever and τ's "fingerprints corrupt the trunk" story weakens. If the policy stays degraded (≳1.0) despite the 5×-weaker gradient, then even a small dose of fingerprint gradient is harmful — the *content* story — and τ's value is specifically that it removes the fingerprints. A partial recovery means both mechanisms contribute. Note the comparison points: this cell reads against the 06-05 n20 baseline (λ=0.5, hard CE, val_policy 1.039) and the λ=0 run (0.9615), not against the anchor.

**prepre_drop / skip_drop.** The anchor places its channel dropout *after* the pyramid pre-module, which means the 0.82M-param module sees clean trunk features and is free to build fingerprint detectors internally — the dropout only noises their output. These two cells test the two mechanistically-motivated alternatives: `prepre_drop` moves the dropout to the module's input (so it can't rely on exact channel combinations at all), and `skip_drop` moves it onto the skip connections (the full-resolution shortcut around the 8×8 bottleneck — the route that carries exactly the kind of spatially-precise detail a fingerprint needs, and that placement signal shouldn't need). If either cell pulls the gap clearly below the anchor band, placement matters and the "leftover memorization lives in the pre-module" hypothesis gains support. skip_drop makes a further checkable prediction: it should *keep* the global signal (survivor counting flows through the bottleneck) while losing the train-set advantage — the stratified dumps can confirm both halves. If neither cell moves the gap, either the memorization lives upstream in the trunk (which redirects attention to λ and weight decay) or dropout is simply a minor lever once τ is doing the containment. One caution: these are gap reads — the noisiest metric we have — at a single seed, so a decisive conclusion here will likely need the targeted replicates.

**tau0.3.** Lowering τ sharpens the targets, which raises the payoff for memorizing them (at τ=0.3, fully memorizing the soft labels gets the train loss most of the way back to what hard CE allowed). So this cell probes the boundary: if the gap re-inflates past 1, containment depends on τ's strength and 0.6 is close to the working edge — useful to know before anyone tunes τ downward for sharper value predictions. If it stays tamed (gap ~0.7, still below the floor), containment is robust across the useful range and τ's exact value is a free parameter — consistent with the robustness Farebrother et al. report for their Gaussian version of the same idea.

**tau1.0.** The measurement trap to preregister around: our reported `value` metric is always hard CE, and a τ=1.0 head pays ~0.46 nats on that metric *even if it knows the answer with certainty* (because it's trained to emit softened targets, and the hard-CE measurement penalizes the softness itself). So its val_value and Δ-vs-floor will look mechanically worse, and judging it on those would be unfair by construction. Read it instead on the gap (memorization), the policy (trunk health), and the stratified *directions* (where it beats its conditional floors). If those improve, heavier softening buys something real; if they're flat and only the calibration tax grows, τ=0.6 was already enough.

Cross-cutting reads, applying to every cell: does any configuration move the value head's val optimum to the final epoch (the success criterion adopted for these tweaks — it would dissolve the best-value vs best-policy checkpoint tension), and what the per-epoch dumps show about each config's erosion shape after its optimum.

Sweep dir (resolved configs + preregistration notes): `data/training/sweeps/2026-06-12-valuehead-wave-a`

**A deliberate trade-off, recorded:** consolidating into a single launch put the τ cells on the *anchor* placement rather than staging them on the placement verdict. The τ mechanism question is largely orthogonal and the consolidation gains a sweep-cycle — but if a placement cell wins decisively, the τ boundary may want re-checking at the new placement.
