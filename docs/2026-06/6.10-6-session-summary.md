# Session summary — value-head lever results and synthesis

Date: 2026-06-10

Results session for the 6.10-3 launches. Companion docs: [`6.10-4`](6.10-4-value-head-levers-results.md) (results reference: tables, reproduction commands, analysis breadcrumbs) and [`6.10-5`](6.10-5-value-head-synthesis.md) (interpretation, mechanism picture, next lines).

## Headline findings (details and confidence grading in 6.10-5)

- **The trunk-corruption hypothesis held.** The preregistered λ=0 discriminator landed on its corruption branch (val_policy 0.9615 at n20); the τ cells recover policy equally well *while still training the head*; the full-24k lever run beats the no-lever baseline at every matched epoch and posts the best policy numbers of any run to date (0.8347 / 74.9% top-1). Dense history shows no policy harm once the head is tamed.
- **First value heads ever to beat the global floor** (0-for-15 before this). τ is the load-bearing lever; head dropout stacks on top; dropout alone fails. All n5 cells still collapse to the prior — now the sharpest open puzzle, since the survivor-counting features live in the base obs shared across n.
- **What the tamed head knows:** survivor-counting + within-bucket priors (robust), plus a small real late-game signal at the val optimum (~2σ tier) that mostly erodes if training continues — the post-optimum dynamic is a uniform retreat of prediction confidence toward bucket priors. Best-value (ep2) and best-policy (ep6) checkpoints now diverge.
- **Memorization is damped and decoupled, not eliminated** — the train/val value gap is still ~0.7 nats and ~scale-invariant under the levers (two-point observation).
- An 80-game heads-up eval (user-run) between the lever n5/n20 checkpoints came out dead even — consistent with the corruption-fixed story, underpowered for small edges.

## Most promising next steps (discussion menu, argued in 6.10-5)

Trunk-vs-readout probe (frozen-trunk head retrain; the λ=0 checkpoint is a natural control), the dense-history rematch at 24k with levers, a seed replicate to calibrate every n=1 comparison, λ/τ/dropout annealing (cheap first arm: resume the bonus run with per-epoch dumps), and — the bet aimed at making the signal large rather than safe — denser per-frame targets from the 6.09-1 aux-head menu.
