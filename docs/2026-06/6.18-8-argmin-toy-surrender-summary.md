# Argmin toy: surrender slice — results summary

*Date: 2026-06-18. Status: findings (Phase 0c). Operational quick-reference for the argmin-toy surrender slice. Full walkthrough, per-cell grid, and caveats in [6.18-7](6.18-7-argmin-toy-surrender-results-walkthru.md).*

## Bottom line

Prod's capture-based status channel is **information-equivalent to raw army** for excluding surrendered players — provable by construction (captured ⟹ `army==0`) and confirmed empirically. Only a true alive mask fixes it.

## Results (trustworthy legs; `cloud_24k_sub_5k` val)

Surrender-subpop ceiling: **63.2%** under `alive`, **100%** under `army_pos`.

| Encoding (leg) | `alive` surr / full | `army_pos` surr |
|---|---|---|
| none (E1_mlp ×3) | **63.2** / 97.6 | 100 |
| capture_status (E1_mlp ×3) | **63.2** / 97.6 | 100 |
| boolean (D1, E1_linear) | **100** / 100 | — |
| categorical (D1, E1_linear) | **100** / 100 | — |

- `none ≡ capture_status`: identical to the digit, 3 seeds → channel adds nothing.
- `boolean`/`categorical`: 100% on surr *linearly* → alive mask excludes, no ceiling.
- target flip `none`: 63.2% → 100% → surrender-exclusion is the whole cap.

## So what (elim-head)

- **"next DeathEvent" target** (exclude surrendered): prod's status inputs **can't support it** — `capture_status` is blind to surrender, no alive channel. Would need a surrender-aware alive channel added to obs.
- **"next board-removal" target** (include surrendered): army alone suffices.
- Evidence *for*, not a decision *on*, the target choice ([6.18-4 §10](6.18-4-argmin-toy-surrender-design.md)).
