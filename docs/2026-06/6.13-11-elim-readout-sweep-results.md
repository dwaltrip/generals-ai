# Elim-head readout/structure A/B — pool and capacity surface bin-0, don't rescue bin-1

**Date:** 2026-06-13

**Description:** results + synthesis for the elim-head readout/structure sweep that tests hypotheses #2 (pool dilution) and #4 (head capacity) from [`6.13-8`](6.13-8-elim-weighting-sweep-results.md)'s *"What flat imminent precision does and doesn't tell us"*. Head knobs (`elim_pool`, `elim_head_hidden`) are the config-gated additions from this session; design in [`6.13-5`](6.13-5-next-elimination-head-design.md). Sweep: [`data/training/sweeps/2026-06-13-elim-head-readout`](../../data/training/sweeps/2026-06-13-elim-head-readout) (gitignored — this doc is the durable record).

A 2×2 factorial, all cells single-seed, sub_5k, n20, 5 epochs, seed 0, on a **new `lambda_value=0.1` baseline** (off the long-standing 0.5 default; `0.1 + tau 0.6` is the config-of-record Wave A closure #1 flagged as never run). Both knobs ride `lambda_elim=0.15`, `elim_target_tau=1.0`, default 8-bin edges, unweighted.

- `elim_pool`: **mean** (masked global avg, v1) vs **lse** (masked length-normalized log-sum-exp, learnable softplus temperature β; β→0 recovers mean, β→∞ recovers max). The lever for #2 — the mean pool dilutes spatially-localized imminent-death evidence (~3 of ~200 tiles ≈ a 1.5% perturbation of the mean).
- `elim_head_hidden`: **0** (single linear readout conv, v1) vs **192** (Conv(C→192)→ReLU pre-pool). The lever for #4 — whether the danger feature is nonlinear in trunk channels and needs a hidden layer to form.

Cell `mean-1conv` is the off×off baseline; `lse-2conv` is both levers on.

## The short version

The readout tweaks do something, but not the thing we needed. **Each single lever (lse *or* capacity) lifts the precision of the bin we could already read — bin-0 `[0,10)`, ~21.7% → ~30% — but neither rescues bin-1 `[10,20)`, which stays at near-chance precision and ~zero prediction-frequency. The bin-0/bin-1 split does not close.** lse raises the head's *willingness* to bet imminent (pred-freq up) without raising its *accuracy* (precision flat) — the flat-PR-curve, no-separable-signal signature from 6.13-8. The two levers also **don't stack**: combined (`lse-2conv`) they fall back to ≈baseline on bin-0, sub-additive to the point of apparent cancellation. Do-no-harm to policy/value holds throughout. Net: #2 and #4 are now tested and neither is the bin-1 bottleneck, so the live explanations narrow to **#1 (data scale)** and **#3 (under-carved trunk)** — which a head-structure tweak cannot separate.

## Settled findings

**1. Do-no-harm holds (within single-seed noise).**
- The four readout variants are indistinguishable on policy: `v_pol` 0.9320–0.9353 / 72.3–72.4% top1 by ep5 (spread 0.003), value CE best 1.286–1.333 with no trend across variants.
- vs the elim-OFF `value-lam0.1` cell ([`2026-06-13-elim-head-stage1`](../../data/training/sweeps/2026-06-13-elim-head-stage1), best v_val 1.335 @1, v_pol 0.9303): the elim-on cells sit ≤0.005 higher on `v_pol` — at the edge of the single-seed band, no systematic cost from turning the head on at lam0.1.

**2. Each single lever lifts bin-0 precision (~21.7% → ~30%); the combination does not stack.**
- ep3 bin-0 precision: 21.7 (baseline) → 30.4 (lse) / 30.6 (capacity) → 22.9 (both). ep5 repeats the ordering: 16.3 → 23.9 / 23.9 → 18.4.
- This is the firmest cross-cell result: bin-0 precision is computed over ~1,500 predictions (pred-freq ~0.3–0.5% of ~513k alive pairs), so within-cell count noise is ~±1% — the 21.7→30 lift and the combination's drop back to baseline both clear it. (Whether the *cancellation* is a real interaction or seed noise is NOT settled — see hypotheses.)

**3. bin-1 is not rescued by any variant; the bin-0/bin-1 split does not close.**
- bin-1 precision stays 8.6–11.7% (ep3) / 6.1–12.5% (ep5) vs bin-0's 22–31%, at pred-freq ~0.0–0.3% everywhere — the head won't bet imminent on it.
- lse raises *willingness*, not *accuracy*: pred-freq 0.1%→0.3% (lse-1conv) at precision flat ~10% — sliding along a flat PR curve, the no-separable-signal signature (6.13-8's prior-vs-likelihood lens).
- Confusion view (`lse-1conv` ep3, the *best* bin-1 cell): of 14,699 true-bin-1 player·frames, only 181 (1.2%) are predicted bin-1; 78% land in bins 3/4/5/never (predicted "≥40 ticks / safe-or-never"). True imminent-death players look safe to the head.

**4. #2 (readout dilution) and #4 (head capacity) are tested; neither is the bin-1 bottleneck.**
- `elim_pool=lse` and `elim_head_hidden=192` are exactly the levers for #2 and #4 (6.13-5 / 6.13-8); both fail to move bin-1 off the floor (finding 3). By elimination, the live 6.13-8 explanations for the bin-1 wall are #1 (data scale) and #3 (under-carved trunk).
- Not a *clean* null for #2: lse did lift bin-0 and nudged bin-1 willingness, so the mean pool *was* a dilutant — just not *the* limiter for bin-1.

**5. The elim head barely beats its own floor.**
- val_elim_soft 1.902–1.925 vs the 1.9336 soft-marginal floor — a 0.01–0.03 nat margin. The head learns little beyond predicting the bin marginal, consistent with 6.13-8's read that only the easy "never"/survivor-counting signal is genuinely learned at sub_5k. Capacity cells own the larger margins (1.902/1.907), the faint aggregate edge behind finding 2 — but it is within single-seed noise and does not translate to bin-1.

## Hypotheses (not settled)

- **The non-stacking is sub-additive saturation; the cancellation may be real interference or seed noise.** Saturation is expected — lse (fixes aggregation: peak not divided by board size) and capacity (sharpens the per-cell feature, a bigger peak that survives mean-pool better) push the *same* quantity (danger-vs-background contrast in the pooled logit), so they're substitutes hitting bin-0's intrinsic ceiling. Combination *below* both singles needs more: candidate causes are over-flexibility overfitting on sub_5k, over-concentration losing useful spatial averaging, or plain seed noise (n=1, imminent-bin deltas are ~1–2σ). Undetermined without seed replicates.
- **bin-1 is limited by #1 or #3, not the readout.** The cleanest reading of findings 3–4. Which of #1/#3 is open and is what the next session resolves.

## What "bin-1 rescued" would look like (success criterion)

The next session needs this crisp, because "precision 11% at 0.3% pred-freq" is *not* it.

- **Precision that holds as pred-freq/recall rise** — the PR curve bowing *outward*, not the operating point sliding along a flat one. The tell is precision staying high (say 25–40%) *while* pred-freq climbs toward the base rate (~2–3%) and recall climbs off the floor. We saw the opposite: lse pushed pred-freq up, precision stayed ~10%.
- **bin-1 looking like bin-0 instead of the noise bins** — a precision multiple over base toward bin-0's 8–10× (vs bin-1's current ~3×).
- **The confusion *row* going diagonal** — true-bin-1 concentrating on predicted bins 0/1/2 (the soft-ordinal neighborhood), not smeared into 4/5/never as it is now.

## #1 data scale vs #3 under-carved trunk, and the disambiguation ladder

Both are "the bin-1 feature isn't readable," but they locate the limit differently:

- **#1 data scale** — the limit is in the *dataset*: too few learnable bin-1 instances in 5k games to estimate "board state → death in 10–20 ticks" at all. Fix: more data (24k). The concept is learnable, there just aren't enough examples.
- **#3 under-carved trunk** — the limit is in the *representation*: the data may hold the signal, but the trunk hasn't formed death-timing features. It's shaped mostly by policy (weight 1) + pass (1), with elim a weak 0.15 out-competed by move-prediction gradients. Fix: more elim pressure (higher λ_elim, more epochs) on the same data.

Crisp version: **#1 = "is the signal in the data?" (fix: more examples); #3 = "did training extract it into features, given this data?" (fix: more elim gradient).**

Ladder to separate them, cheapest first:

1. **Frozen-trunk-vs-readout probe** (cheap; technique in [`6.10-5`](6.10-5-value-head-synthesis.md), sketched there for the value head). Freeze the current trunk, train a fresh fat head hard on bin-1. Extracts bin-1 → feature was already there, readout under-read it (back to #2/#4). Can't → feature isn't in the current trunk — but this alone can't split #1 from #3 (could be data-lacks-it OR λ=0.15-never-carved-it).
2. **Heavy elim pressure on sub_5k** (high λ_elim, more epochs, same data). bin-1 now learns → **#3** (carvable, was under-pressured). Still won't → leans **#1** (data-limited), not conclusive.
3. **24k** (expensive). bin-1 learns at scale when sub_5k pressure couldn't → **#1** (data scale). Still won't with real pressure → deeper (the target/concept itself).

The order matters: #3's fix is cheap (crank λ/epochs on data we have), and ruling it out *before* the 24k run prevents over-attributing a 24k win to scale when it was really under-carving.

## Connections to recent work

- **The 24k transfer thread is distinct and still open.** That thread asks "does the elim head *enrich the trunk*?" (the value head's within-bucket stratified read, with vs. without the elim head — 6.13-5 success criterion); this sweep measured *head quality* (its own prediction precision). They can conflict. The ladder interacts with it: the probe / heavy-pressure step informs whether 24k is worth running and how to read it. Sequencing between the ladder and the 24k transfer run is deliberately unresolved. Stage-2 baseline candidate remains the 6.10 24k run (`data/training/runs-cloud/2026-06-10T09-20-33Z`, 6.13-8 §Stage-2).
- **The value-head meta-pattern (Wave A, [`6.12-4`](6.12-4-wave-a-results-and-synthesis.md)).** Both heads learn survivor-*counting* but not survivor-*ranking*. The bin-1 wall here is the elim analog of the value head's within-bucket wall — and both heads read the trunk through pooling, so a shared-readout bottleneck would produce the same wall. This sweep weakly tests that for elim: a better readout helped bin-0 but not bin-1, so the shared wall is more likely signal/representation than pooling. The denser, ranking-shaped target was the whole aux bet — still genuinely unresolved, and what Stage 2 exists to test.
- **The PR-curve / prior-vs-likelihood lens ([`6.13-8`](6.13-8-elim-weighting-sweep-results.md)).** Weighting = a prior knob (slides the operating point along a fixed curve); a readout fix could in principle bend the curve outward. It bent bin-0's a little and bin-1's not at all — so the lens that diagnosed weighting now diagnoses the readout: flat curve = nothing to surface.

## Open decisions / next moves

Not prescribed — for the next session to walk with the user:

- **Which ladder rung, in what order.** My lean: the **probe first** (cheapest, isolates trunk-vs-readout cleanly), then heavy-pressure-on-sub_5k to split #1/#3, holding 24k until those read out. But the probe's negative is partly ambiguous (a mean-pool-trained trunk may not have localized the signal), so reasonable to go straight to heavy-pressure if a single decisive read is wanted.
- **Sequencing vs the 24k transfer run** — the distinct thread. 24k could be launched in parallel (it answers a different question) or gated behind the ladder (so its read isn't confounded by under-carving). Open.
- **Whether to spend seeds on the cancellation question** — I'd lean no: the interaction isn't the result we care about (bin-1 not lifting is), and it'd cost a fresh sweep.

## Tooling note

The new instrumentation carried this analysis cleanly:
- `compare_runs --cols ...,elim` — the optional elim column group (t_/v_elim, e_top1, e_H), presence-gated, did the do-no-harm + health read in one command.
- `stratified ... report` now prints per-bin **precision + pred-freq** beside recall, and `--confusion` prints the 8×8 C[true,pred] matrix — the precision read 6.13-8 did by hand. Both reduce from one importable primitive (`training/analysis/elim_metrics.py`: `confusion_counts` → `per_bin_metrics`).

The one gap: the *cross-cell* per-bin precision comparison (the table above) has no CLI — it's a single-run tool by design, so I `awk`'d the per-bin table out of 4 separate `report` runs and assembled by hand. A small cross-run per-bin-precision view (a mode, or a script on the `elim_metrics` primitive) is the candidate improvement if these readout sweeps recur — and Stage 2's with/without read will want it.
