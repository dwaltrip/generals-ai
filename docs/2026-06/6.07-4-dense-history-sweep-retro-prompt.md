## Intro and Session Goal

A few days ago, we finished our first dense-history sweep for `n`, using values n=5,10,20. The `n` here refers to the numberr of previous ticks for which we show detailed per-tile tactical info (ownership changes and army movements).

My goal for this session is to analyze these results and discuss the implications and improvements I might want to make next, based on what we learn and synthesize.
