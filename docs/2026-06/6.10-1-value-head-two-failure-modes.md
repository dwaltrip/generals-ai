## Historical metrics pass (15 realistic runs)

`compare_runs --summary-only` over the 6.09-4 inventory, now with floors and gap columns:

- **No run has ever beaten its floor.** Δbest (best `val_value` − manifest floor) is positive in all 15 runs, every head variant, every data scale. The value head has never demonstrably extracted board signal beyond the placement marginals — though see the collapse finding below for why the aggregate can't distinguish "honest at floor" from "learned nothing".
- **`direct` is not a safe landing spot.** It memorizes at gaps of 1.15–1.9 everywhere above the 2k PoC — pyramid's range. In the matched pairs, direct's gap is comparable or worse (sub_5k: direct 1.55 vs pyramid 1.29; 24k/bs256: 1.90 vs 1.81). Head size between these two variants barely moves memorization; the 6.09-1 shrink-the-head caveat was warranted.
- **Effective data scale dominates the gap**: direct at sub_5k 1.55 → 24k 1.15; the two half-data-per-epoch bs=256 runs are the worst two gaps outside the sweep, one per variant.
- The n5 sweep leg was the only no-gap, at-floor run — see below for what that actually was.

## The collapse finding (n5 sweep leg, epoch 3)

The stratified dump shows the n5 value head outputs a **numerically constant distribution** — per-frame prediction-entropy std 0.000000, per-class prob std ~1e-4 — and that constant is approximately the placement marginal (0.493/0.206/0.115/... vs val marginal 0.513/0.211/0.142/...). Per-bucket value CE follows mechanically: each bucket's floor plus the KL between the bucket's placement distribution and the global marginal, worst in the 2-alive bucket (+0.33 over its 0.652 conditional floor) because late-game placement diverges most from the marginal.

Two readings this kills:

- "n5's value head was healthy." It was at floor *by collapse to the prior* — zero state-dependence, zero trunk-shaping, zero advantage signal for a future critic. The failure mode 6.09-1 warned aggregate floor-matching can't detect, caught on the collapse check's first use.
- "Still improving at epoch 3." Under constant output, `val_value` only improves by the constant drifting toward the empirical marginal. The apparent learning was the head annealing its prior (inference from the constant-output fact, not separately measured — the killed epoch-1/2 dumps would show the trajectory).

Method note — **conditional floors**: each bucket is scored against the placement entropy *within* that bucket, a stronger baseline than the global marginal since it credits the model only for signal beyond bucket membership (2-alive ⇒ placement ∈ {1st, 2nd}, floor ≈ ln 2). Caveat: this assumes bucket membership (players alive) is recoverable from the obs — see open question below.

## Revised sweep story: two attractors

The dense-history sweep's value heads didn't fail one way, they fell into two different attractors: **collapse to the prior** (n5) and **fingerprint memorization** (n10/n20, gaps 1.4/1.3). A coherent mechanism: the constant solution is a strong local CE optimum against a per-game fixed label; escaping it requires features that beat the marginal. At n=5 the head apparently never found any; at n=10/20 the history channels supplied features that did beat it — trajectory fingerprints.

Consequence for the trunk-corruption hypothesis: a collapsed head emits ~zero gradient, so the n5 trunk effectively trained without value pressure — an accidental quasi-control, close to a natural λ≈0 arm. The sweep's policy ordering (n5 0.965 < n10 0.985 < n20 1.039) then tracks how active each run's value gradient was, exactly the corruption story's prediction. This is supporting-but-not-discriminating evidence: the channel-count confound is intact (more history channels could harm policy optimization directly), and the fingerprinting could live substantially in the pyramid head's own 0.82M params rather than the trunk. Both rivals fall to the same experiment, which now carries a sharp quantitative prediction: **λ=0 (or detached value gradient) at n=20 should recover val_policy to roughly the n5 level (~0.97) if the corruption story holds; staying near the n20 baseline (~1.04) implicates the channels themselves.**

## The fix landscape, reframed

The problem is now two-sided: the head must **get past the marginal** (escape collapse by extracting real signal) *without* sliding into fingerprint memorization. The two attractors sit on either side of the goal, so any single-metric success criterion is gameable — a regularizer that "fixes" memorization can land in collapse and look like victory on the gap column.

**Win condition (measurable, via the new tooling): below-conditional-floor in late-game buckets, with varying predictions (prediction entropy moves across states).**

Candidate levers, grouped by mechanism, with priors updated by this session's evidence:

- **Head capacity** — `direct` is downgraded (memorizes like pyramid, per the historical pass). GAP+linear (~1k params) is untested at scale and structurally can't store much, but may also be too weak to escape the marginal. Input bottleneck (project trunk features to a few channels before the head) is the same family.
- **Gradient path to trunk** — lower λ, λ annealing (value pressure early, damped when memorization typically onsets, around epoch 2), full detach (stop-grad: eliminates corruption by construction, doubles as the diagnostic; forfeits positive trunk-shaping), two-tower separate value trunk (the architectural endpoint; legitimate for PPO). These cap the damage but do nothing for getting past the marginal.
- **Target-side** — label smoothing (one-liner, damps confident memorization); ordinal/soft placement targets (placement is ordinal — spreading mass to adjacent placements injects real structure the 8-way CE ignores); **denser per-frame targets** (the 6.09-1 aux-head menu: survival horizon, next elimination, etc.). Denser targets are the only lever here that attacks *both* attractors — a per-frame-varying target makes the constant solution much weaker while reducing the payoff of per-game fingerprints. Strongest candidate on the get-past-the-marginal side; real pipeline work.
- **Input-side** — symmetry augmentation (dihedral board transforms + matching policy-target transforms): attacks fingerprinting precisely (a game stops having a single memorizable key, effective game count ×~8), helps every head, zero params; real pipeline work to get exactly right. Coarsened/decayed history channels (fewer unique signatures; confounds the dense-history question). **Possible new lever:** opponent-aliveness/elimination obs channels — see open question.
- **Data scale** — the proven gap-diluter (historical pass), with ~7× more corpus on disk than cloud_24k uses. Dilutes memorization; unknown whether scale alone gets a head past the marginal (no run at any scale has).

## Open questions / next steps

- **Is `players_alive` recoverable from the obs?** Eliminations are announced in the real game, but whether our 96-channel encoding carries opponent-aliveness is unverified, and fog memory may keep dead opponents' remembered tiles around. If it's absent, part of the collapse is an input gap, and aliveness/elimination channels become a cheap, well-motivated obs addition. Check `build_obs` first.
- **Remaining dumps** (slate was killed after the n5 ep3 result, by design — review point): the n20-24kfull dumps are most informative next — does the memorizing head vary on val (confidently wrong) or also flatline? Its ep2-vs-ep4 contrast shows whether memorization destroyed real late-game signal or there was never any. n5 ep1–2 would show the collapse trajectory. `--sample-frac 0.25` makes the big run ~10 min/checkpoint.
- **The discriminating run**: λ=0 or detach at n=20 on cloud_24k, against the existing n20 baseline, with the ~0.97 prediction above. Needs `LAMBDA_VALUE` threaded into config (small change).
- **Fix-run readouts**: any value-head intervention (dropout, smoothing, etc.) gets a stratified dump as its success criterion, not the gap column alone.
- The n=5 @ cloud_24k full baseline run (zero code change) remains queued — it serves the dense-history question independent of the value-head work.
