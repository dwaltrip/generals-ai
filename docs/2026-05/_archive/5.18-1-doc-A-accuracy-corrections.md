### M2. [done] `opp_N_has_seen` cost framing — cheap, not "walk every player's vision"

**Location:** §4.2.1 ("Online fog computation"), the per-cell bullet list.

**Current:**
> 7 × `opp_N_has_seen` masks — opp N's Moore neighborhood ever covered this cell. Requires walking *every player's* vision per timestep, not just our own. Expensive? Cheap? Not obvious at the napkin level.

**Issue:** The derivation rule means the channel is updated only from our own observation of opp-N tiles, not from any walk over every player's vision.

**Source — `5.05-1` §3.4.2:**

> Per-opponent binary spatial masks. For each opponent N, 1 at any cell the agent has ever observed to be within opponent N's Moore-neighborhood vision range. Monotonically increasing.
>
> **Derivation:** whenever the agent sees a tile owned by opponent N, all 8 neighbors of that tile plus the tile itself are marked as "seen by opponent N."

So the per-timestep update is: scan tiles within *our own* current vision, find opp-N-owned ones, dilate each by 3×3, OR into the running mask. O(my-vision-size) per opponent per timestep. Cheap.

**Proposed fix:** Replace with framing that matches the derivation rule:

> 7 × `opp_N_has_seen` masks — for each cell C, "did I ever observe opp N owning a tile whose Moore neighborhood includes C?" Cheap to compute online: each timestep, look at opp-N-owned tiles within our own current vision and dilate by 3×3 into the running mask. O(my-vision-size) per opponent per timestep.

The §4.3.4 / §9 decision to drop these 7 channels for spike v1 still stands — but on "minimal marginal value at this corpus size for the spike" grounds, not "expensive to compute." §8's framing ("expensive vision walk") in the bullet about dropping channels for v1 should also be edited to "marginal-value drop, not cost drop."
