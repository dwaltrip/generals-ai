## Open decisions (need your call)

These are the ones I'm not pre-empting:

1. **Value head — fix in spike or punt to Phase 4?** The dead-ReLU diagnosis sharpens this. Tier-1 structural fixes in `5.21-1` include one-line changes (remove ReLU, swap to LeakyReLU, widen conv to K>1) that could realistically fit in-spike. The reinit-and-retrain probe is also cheap. Tier 2 (turn-gate, frame-weight, bootstrapped targets) is moot until Tier 1 unsticks the head, so the spike-vs-Phase-4 split now lands more naturally between Tier 1 and Tier 2 than between "do" and "don't."

2. **Shuffle-buffer (chunk 6) in spike or Phase 4?** Within-game frame correlation is a plausible amplifier of the value-head pathology. If we don't include chunk 6, we can't rule that out. If we do, it pushes spike scope.

3. **Phase 3 success bar.** Define it now (post-hoc justify what we got), or accept "we shipped the pipeline + found a thing" as the success criteria?

4. **Stale `known_general` flag fix** — B or C? Cheap fix, real correctness issue, but small-effect. Test is already pinned to wait for the fix.
